EmeraldCast: watch multiple Twitch streams at once

EmeraldCast is a free multi-stream viewer for Twitch. Open as many streams as you want side by side, rearrange them with drag-and-drop, pick which stream plays audio, and pin any stream's chat. No account required.

  • Multi-stream grid
  • Main + sidebar layout
  • Per-stream chat & audio focus
  • Merged chat across streams
  • Shareable stream links
